Parkinson’s Awareness

Recognizing Possible Early Signs

Parkinson’s disease can affect movement, speech, balance, and daily activities. Some changes may develop gradually and can initially be overlooked.

Voice Changes

Speech may become softer, lower, or less clear over time.

Hand Tremors

Shaking may appear in the hand, fingers, or limbs while resting.

Slower Movement

Everyday movements may gradually become slower or more difficult.

Balance Changes

Posture, stability, and coordination may become affected.

Facial Expression

Facial movements may become less noticeable or expressive.

Daily Activities

Writing, walking, or routine tasks may require more effort.
